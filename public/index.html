Why VitaBite

Personal nutrition
should feel personal.

We’re surrounded by nutrition advice, but very little of it fits real people — or the places where we actually eat.

VitaBite is a concept for making evidence-informed food choices easier to understand and put into practice. We’re exploring how AI could connect individual preferences, nutrition information, and the menus and products people encounter every day.
